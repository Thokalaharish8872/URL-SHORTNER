# Module 1 Quick Pass: Requirements Extraction

## 5 Explicit Requirements

1. Users can browse providers by category
2. Users can view provider profiles with ratings
3. Users can book time slots and pay through the platform
4. Providers can set their own availability, pricing, and descriptions
5. Platform takes 15% commission on provider earnings

## 2 Ambiguities (Least Sure About)

1. "Feel instant" search - what latency threshold under what load?
2. "At least a few thousand users" - what is the actual target scale?

## 1 Question to Ask PM Right Now

Does the 15% commission apply uniformly to all service categories, or does it vary by category?
