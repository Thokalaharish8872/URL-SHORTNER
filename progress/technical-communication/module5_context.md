# Module 5 Context: Stakeholder Communication

## Micro-Exercise Answers

**Part 1: Explain database migration to a baker friend (no technology words)**
Imagine all your recipe cards, customer orders, and inventory lists are stored in one big filing cabinet in the back office. The cabinet is old and disorganized. Moving to a new cabinet means taking everything out of the old one and putting it in a new, better-organized cabinet. While you're moving, you can't look anything up - the shop pauses for a few hours. Once it's done, everything is in the new cabinet and you can look things up again.

**Part 2: Explain WHY you would do it (not HOW)**
The old cabinet is too small and disorganized. When the Saturday morning rush hits, it takes you 30 seconds to find a recipe instead of 3. Customers wait in line longer, and some get impatient and leave. The new cabinet is bigger, organized so the recipes you use most are right at the front, and you can find things instantly. Moving to the new cabinet means faster service during rushes and fewer customers walking away.
